Tomorrow's workout

35-minute upper-body session

Train bench and upper back without meaningfully loading the sore squat muscles or provoking the spasms.

35 minutes · Controlled upper-body work · Finish feeling the same or slightly looser

Rule for the whole session

Discomfort from changing position is acceptable only if it settles immediately. Stop any exercise that produces a spasm, sharp pain, leg weakness, or progressively worsening discomfort.

If the warm-up itself repeatedly provokes spasms, convert the session to walking and gentle mobility only.

0:00–6:00 — Gentle warm-up

Move continuously but unhurriedly.

  1. Easy walk around the room — 2 minutes.
    Walk timer02:00

    Ready.

  2. Shoulder circles — 10 each direction.
  3. Band pull-aparts — 2 sets of 10.
  4. Wall slides — 1 set of 8.
  5. Very shallow supported bodyweight squats — 1 set of 5. Hold the rack firmly and descend only as far as feels relieving. These are a movement check, not training. Skip them entirely if they trigger a spasm.

Do not include: kettlebell swings, unsupported hip hinges, or your usual squat warm-up.

6:00–21:00 — Bench press

Use a restrained setup: minimal arch; feet placed comfortably rather than pulled far underneath you; little or no forceful leg drive; a controlled descent; and no grinding repetitions.

Bench warm-up

about 6 minutes
  1. Empty bar × 10
  2. 40 kg × 5
  3. 50 kg × 5
  4. 60 kg × 3

Rest approximately 60–90 seconds between sets.

Warm-up rest01:00

Ready.

Bench work sets

3 × 5 at 65 kg

Rest 2 minutes between sets. Each set should finish with approximately 3 repetitions in reserve. Reduce to 60 kg if positioning, leg tension, or getting off the bench aggravates the soreness.

Getting up: roll gently onto your side first rather than performing a forceful sit-up.

Work-set rest02:00

Ready.

21:00–30:00 — Chest-supported dumbbell row

Chest-supported dumbbell row

3 × 8–10

Set the bench to a comfortable incline and support your chest fully. Choose a weight that feels like approximately 6–7 out of 10 effort.

Cues: keep the chest against the bench; do not push hard through the feet; pull the elbows towards the hips; pause briefly at the top; and lower under control.

Avoid: bent-over rows, Pendlay rows, and unsupported one-arm rows tomorrow.

Row rest01:30

Ready.

30:00–34:00 — Upper-back and shoulder finish

Perform two relaxed rounds:

  • Band face pulls × 12
  • Band external rotations × 10 each side
  • Dead hang × 10–20 seconds

Skip the dead hang if mounting or dismounting the bar requires an uncomfortable jump or abrupt leg movement.

Finish block04:00

Ready.

34:00 onward — Recovery finish

  1. Walk gently for 1 minute.
  2. Hold the rack and perform 5 shallow, slow squat movements, only if they feel better rather than worse.
  3. Finish with the gentle stretch or light massage technique that has already been relieving the spasms.
