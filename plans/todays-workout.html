Today's workout

Show up. Stay controlled. Finish with more in reserve.

Today is a baseline, not a test. Use smooth, comfortable reps and stop each set while your technique is still crisp.

About 35 minutes · Target effort RPE 5–6/10 · No maximal work

Safety first

Stop the exercise for sharp or increasing pain, a painful shoulder pinch, joint instability or giving way, new numbness or weakness, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath. Do not work through symptoms that change your movement.

Seek assessment from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional if symptoms are severe, persist, recur, or limit normal activity. This workout is general training guidance, not medical advice or a diagnosis.

1. Warm-up — 6 minutes

  1. Easy march, walk, or bike — 2 minutes.
  2. Gentle neck turns — 5 each side, only through a comfortable range; do not force the stiff direction.
  3. Shoulder-blade squeezes — 8 slow reps, arms relaxed.
  4. Ankle rocks holding support — 8 each side, heel down.
  5. Box squats to a comfortable height — 6 easy reps.

Readiness check: if the warm-up makes symptoms worse rather than easier, switch to a comfortable walk or stop for the day.

2. Conservative baseline

Rest about one minute between sets. Choose loads that leave roughly 4–5 good reps in reserve. The goal is repeatable work, not fatigue.

3. Finish — 3 minutes

  • Easy walk and relaxed breathing — 1 minute.
  • Gentle ankle circles — 5 each direction per side.
  • Comfortable neck turns — 3 each side. Stay well short of forcing range.

Progress rule: repeat this baseline before adding work. If technique stayed crisp and there is no meaningful symptom flare during or after the session, add only one small change next time: a little load, one rep per set, or one set to a pulling exercise.
